# Gate G0 — training mode, GPU types and fallback for the 4B study (one page)

- Date: 2026-11-03
- Milestone: M1 (task M1.12). Rules for later configs: decision 025.
- Decision: Every 4B algorithm (GRPO, Dr.GRPO, DAPO, RS-GRPO, DPO), and the SFT they start from,
  trains in **full BF16** (BF16 compute, FP32 master weights and AdamW) on **4×A40 (fc098),
  confirmed**. When fc098 is unavailable, runs wait; a whole comparison block moves to 2×A100 only
  on your request. Development runs use Qwen3.5-2B-Base.

- **Wall-clock on 4×A40 (full BF16):** 30M tokens 3.2 h, 50M 5.4 h, 100M 10.8 h, 200M 21.5 h,
  each one job; × 1.25 for queueing gives 4.0–26.9 h. At 0.5–1.5× the base model's answer length,
  200M takes 20.5–23.6 h (extrapolated: the fit covers only the lengths that ran).
- **Schedule** ([g0_schedule.json](../reports/g0_schedule.json), inputs in
  `scripts/g0_schedule.yaml`): all Lane A work from M2 to M8 is 9.7–26.2 days with the 1.25
  factor, against the plan's 40–60 days. Lane B becomes the limit. A full evaluation costs 74
  A40-hours: M0's 488M tokens at 1,823 tok/s per A40; an A30 gives 0.83 of that, an A10 0.58
  ([m0_eval_gpu_rates.json](../reports/m0_eval_gpu_rates.json)). Evaluating every ~3 h save is
  74–146 evaluations: 34–68 days on the four A30s and six A10s, 24–48 days if fc096/fc097's four
  A40s join (12 workers, the 16-GPU QOS cap while fc098 trains). At most 4 per run: 34–38 and 24–27
  days. The critical path is 24–51 days with the A40 pairs (24–31 capped), 34–70 without (34–40),
  so with M0–M1's two weeks, SFT data work, gates and write-up the study plausibly ends in about
  8–13 weeks with the A40 pairs, 9–16 without. **Weak:** SFT and DPO times come from the RL update
  rate, Gemma is not measured, and the Lane B pool is a scenario, not a reservation. G1 fixes the
  evaluation cadence.
- **Rollout/trainer gap under FP8 KV (risk 3):** `rollout_corr/kl` 0.00063 per step on 4B (0.0009
  on 2B), steady over 14 steps; rollout correction stays off.
- **2×A100 fallback: not measured.** Full BF16 there would very likely need optimizer offload.
- **What M1 does not show:** whether full BF16 learns better or worse than LoRA at 4B. Nothing in
  M1 measures 4B quality: the M1.11 validation rises (0.605→0.648, 0.656→0.688) are one seed each,
  at different learning rates, and are no comparison. The 2B dev run stays out of conclusions.
  Full BF16 is chosen on speed, host RAM, and so that a sharpening result cannot be blamed on a
  low-rank adapter.

## What this fixes for later milestones

- Mode for all algorithms and for SFT (M2 adds the full-BF16 SFT path); QLoRA/LoRA development-only.
- The shared 4B base config, the frozen image and library versions: decision 025.
- Lane A: 4×A40 on fc098. Lane B: 2B development on the A40 pairs (fc096, fc097; any A40 pair for
  2-GPU smokes); evaluation and preference sampling on 1-GPU A30s, then A10s, then spare A40s.
- Fallback (D13): wait for fc098. On your request, one of these blocks moves whole: M3 screening;
  M4 DPO + M5 seed 1; M6 seed 2; M6 seed 3; M7 ablations and group-size check (with the seed-1 runs
  they are compared with); M8 Gemma. Its hardware is fixed when its first run starts.
- Checkpoints every 6 steps (~3 h) and in-loop validation 128 × 2 at each save (D1, D2, D5).

## What would make us revisit it

- An OOM in the first M2/M3 steps: generation leaves 1.5 GB free. Lower `gpu_memory_utilization`
  to 0.65, then offload the optimizer, each as a decision-025 revision before compared runs start.
- A step > 1.5× slower once SFT lengths are known, or lengths drifting outside 0.5–1.5× in RL.
- A verl/vLLM bug that forces a library change (M1 patched several in place: decisions 015, 020, 024).
- fc098 unavailable for long stretches (one 11 h wait in M1), or A100s unavailable for the fallback.
- Lane B unable to keep up with the checkpoints (the schedule's limit).
