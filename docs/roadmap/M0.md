## Risks and open questions
- FASTER's A100s are the PCIe **40 GB** model (node fc024), which answers the plan's open
  question and makes full-BF16 4B RL on 2×40 GB tight; this weighs on G0 in M1.
- The verifier's current behaviour on percentages (`10\%` vs `10`) and equations (`x=3` vs `3`)
  is pinned in tests but not yet audited.
- maj@k groups answers by exact string; equivalent forms split votes. Decide in M0.5 whether to
  group by verifier equivalence instead.
- The plan names "Qwen3.5-2B" for development; the configs use **Qwen3.5-2B-Base** so 2B mirrors
  the 4B base → SFT → RL pipeline. Confirm.
